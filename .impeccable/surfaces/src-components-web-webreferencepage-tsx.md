# Web Development page surface: `/web` (also `/web-design-company-in-egypt`)

## Visitor mode and job

Persuade business owners and operations leads evaluating a new business website, online store, or service portal. Show the shape of the work, explain how the engagement moves from planning through launch, then make it easy to start an enquiry.

## Direction

Follow the user-provided `assets/images/services/web/total-page-web.png`: a bright, pale-blue and white service page with navy typography, clear blue actions, an integrated laptop-and-phone hero, compact capability links, a simple process line, visual design concepts, verified customer proof, and an enquiry panel. Keep the shared ETripleSoft header and footer.

## Content and proof

Use verified regional office locations and the existing Marco Youssef testimonial. Mark supplied project-preview imagery as illustrative concepts, not delivered client work. Do not reproduce placeholder phone numbers or unsupported customer claims from the reference.

## Responsive behavior

Pair copy and hero artwork on wide screens; stack the hero on narrow screens. Keep service and benefit groups in two columns on phones, reflow the four-step process, and place the contact form below the call to action while retaining its two-column fields where they fit.
