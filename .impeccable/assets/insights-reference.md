# Insights page reference assets

The article illustrations on `/insights` are crops from the user-supplied `assets/images/insights/insights-page.png`. Category marks and labels embedded in the artwork are retained to match the reference; functional filters and metadata are rendered as HTML alongside them.

| Published asset | Source | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `hero-art.webp` | `insights-page.png` | Odoo-themed Insights hero art |
| `featured-article.webp` | `insights-page.png` | Featured article illustration |
| `article-odoo.webp` | `insights-page.png` | Odoo article card |
| `article-erp.webp` | `insights-page.png` | ERP article card |
| `article-ai.webp` | `insights-page.png` | AI article card |
| `article-web.webp` | `insights-page.png` | Web development article card |
| `article-marketing.webp` | `insights-page.png` | Digital marketing article card |
| `article-client-success.webp` | `insights-page.png` | Illustrative Odoo implementation artwork, not a verified client case |

Each WebP has a JSON sidecar recording its crop source and intended use.
